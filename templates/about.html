About Testa Roofing

Honest roofing starts with honest people.

The right roofing solution starts with an honest evaluation. Every recommendation we make begins with one simple question: What is truly best for the customer?

Our Philosophy

Recommend the right solution—not the biggest project.

At Testa Roofing, we believe our responsibility isn't to sell the largest roofing project. It's to recommend the solution that provides the greatest long-term value—whether that's a repair, a restoration, or a complete roof replacement.

That philosophy guides every project we undertake throughout Northeast Ohio and Western Pennsylvania.

Experience From the Roof to the Office

Seeing every side of the project creates better decisions.

Successful roofing projects require much more than quality installation. Accurate estimating, organized project management, communication, scheduling, safety, and budgeting all play an important role in delivering a successful project.

By combining field experience with estimating and project management, we're able to evaluate roofing systems from multiple perspectives before recommending a solution.

That means fewer surprises, more accurate proposals, and realistic expectations from the beginning of every project.

Repair • Restore • Replace

Our approach to roofing.

Whenever possible, we believe a roof should continue serving its purpose through properly completed repairs. When restoration can safely extend the life of an existing roofing system, we'll explain those options before discussing replacement.

Only after those possibilities have been fully evaluated do we recommend installing a completely new roofing system.

We believe honesty creates better roofing decisions—and long-term relationships with our customers.

The Testa Roofing Name

The Testa name has been recognized throughout the Mahoning and Shenango Valleys since 1998.

Since 2017, Testa Roofing has proudly operated under the Absolute Roof Solutions umbrella, continuing that local tradition while building its own reputation through honest evaluations, quality workmanship, and lasting customer relationships.

Build trust through honesty.
