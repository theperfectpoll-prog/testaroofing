Security Response

Recommended steps when unauthorized account activity is suspected.

1
Freeze the account

Immediately stop access to the Testa Roofing administration system.

2
Secure the email account

Change the user's company email password through the GoDaddy / Microsoft 365 administration system.

3
Restore Testa access

Restore the account only after the associated email account has been secured.

4
Reset credentials

Have the administrator request a new password reset before signing back in. MFA will also be re-established when that feature is added.
