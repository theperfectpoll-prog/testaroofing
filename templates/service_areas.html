Service Areas

The right service area for the right type of project.

Based in Youngstown, Ohio, Testa Roofing serves commercial, industrial, and residential customers throughout Northeast Ohio and Western Pennsylvania. Because different roofing services require different response times, our service area varies depending on the type of work.

Commercial Roofing • Restoration • Replacement

Planned roofing projects allow us to serve a broader region.

Larger commercial roofing projects, restorations, replacements, and new construction are typically planned well in advance, allowing us to serve a much larger portion of Northeast Ohio and Western Pennsylvania.

This map represents the primary area where we regularly perform planned commercial roofing projects.

Roof Repairs & Maintenance

Fast response starts with a focused service area.

Roof repairs, leak investigations, emergency service, and preventative maintenance require a much faster response than planned roofing projects. To provide timely service while keeping repair costs reasonable, we concentrate these services closer to our Youngstown office.

By maintaining a smaller repair service area, we're able to respond more quickly and avoid passing unnecessary travel costs on to our customers.

Why Our Service Areas Differ

Different roofing services require different logistics.

Larger roofing projects are scheduled well in advance, making travel practical across a wider geographic area. Roof repairs are often more urgent and involve significantly smaller project values.

By concentrating repair work closer to home while traveling farther for planned commercial projects, we can provide faster response times, better scheduling, and greater overall value.
