Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Overview

Absolute Roof Solutions, doing business as Testa Roofing, respects the privacy of our customers, employees, business partners, website visitors, and authorized users of our administration system.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected through the Testa Roofing website and internal administration system.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Testa Roofing, we may collect information including:

  • Names and company names.
  • Mailing, billing, project, and service addresses.
  • Telephone numbers and email addresses.
  • Customer contacts and project contacts.
  • Estimate, project, work-order, invoice, and payment-related information.
  • Communications and notes related to roofing services.
  • Administrator account and security information used to protect access to the Testa Roofing administration system.

QuickBooks Online Integration

The Testa Roofing administration system may connect with QuickBooks Online to help manage customer and accounting-related information.

When an authorized Testa Roofing user connects QuickBooks Online, the application may access information that is necessary to perform authorized business functions, including customer names, contact information, addresses, accounting identifiers, invoices, payments, and related accounting records.

QuickBooks information is used only for Testa Roofing business operations and is not sold to third parties.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Prepare estimates and proposals.
  • Perform and manage roofing work.
  • Communicate with customers, contractors, property owners, and project contacts.
  • Create and manage work orders.
  • Schedule and track projects.
  • Prepare invoices and record payments.
  • Maintain customer and warranty history.
  • Perform internal business reporting.
  • Protect the security and integrity of Testa Roofing systems.

Information Sharing

Testa Roofing does not sell customer or business information.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the business, provide roofing services, maintain accounting records, host technology systems, or comply with legal obligations.

Data Security

Testa Roofing uses administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Access to the Testa Roofing administration system is restricted to authorized users and may include password protection, multi-factor authentication, account access controls, and security auditing.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, accounting, warranty records, project history, legal obligations, and legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

QuickBooks Disconnection and Data Requests

An authorized Testa Roofing administrator may disconnect the QuickBooks Online integration when it is no longer required.

Requests regarding information maintained by Testa Roofing, including questions concerning correction or deletion when applicable, may be directed to Testa Roofing using the contact information below.
